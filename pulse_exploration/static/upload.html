Measure from a video

Record 30–60 s with the phone's own camera app, then upload the file to the laptop. No install and no certificate needed.

1. Choose what to film

  1. In the camera app pick Video at 1x (the main lens). 1080p at 30 or 60 fps is ideal; 4K or 8K only makes the upload slow. If the app has Auto FPS or stabilisation, turn them off.
  2. Turn the flash on in video mode so the light stays on while recording.
  3. Rest the pad of one fingertip lightly over the main lens and the flash. The picture should turn evenly red. Do not press hard: pressure squeezes the blood out.
  4. Rest your hand and the phone on a table. Hold still and stay quiet for 30–60 s.
  5. Warm hands give a stronger signal.
  1. Use the front camera at arm's length (40–60 cm), with your face filling most of the picture. Prop the phone up if you can.
  2. Turn off beauty, face retouch and filters: they smooth away the tiny colour changes this measures.
  3. Light your face evenly from the front, for example facing a window. Avoid light behind you and flickering lamps.
  4. Sit still, breathe normally and do not talk for 30–60 s. 1080p at 30 fps is plenty.
  5. Face video gives heart rate only, with larger errors than the fingertip.

2. Record or pick a video

Recording from here opens the camera app in a simple mode. For more settings (60 fps, a Pro or manual video mode), record in the camera app first and then choose the file.
