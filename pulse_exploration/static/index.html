Pulse exploration prototype

Measure your pulse with the phone

A research prototype running on the laptop on your local network (this server). It estimates heart rate from the camera. It is not a medical device.

Video upload

tier: video

Nothing to install, no certificate. Record 30–60 s with the phone's own camera app (fingertip on the rear camera with the flash on, or your face on the front camera), then upload the file.

  • Easiest option. Works over plain HTTP.
  • The camera app adjusts exposure and compresses the video, which adds noise. Fingertip video is much more reliable than face video.
  • The whole video file is sent to the laptop.

Live capture

tier: capture

The page drives the camera itself: it turns on the torch and locks exposure and white balance where the phone allows it, then measures the colour of every frame for 45 s.

  • Closest to the setups used in published fingertip studies (about 2–4.5 bpm error).
  • The video never leaves the phone. Only one average colour per frame is sent.
  • Needs HTTPS: Chrome shows a certificate warning once. That is expected here, because the laptop made its own certificate. Tap Advanced, then Proceed.

What to expect

  • Fingertip (rear camera and flash): heart rate is usually within a few bpm. Heart-rate variability is rough at 30 frames per second.
  • Face (front camera): heart rate only, and only when you sit still in even light. Errors of 10 bpm or more are common on compressed phone video. HRV from face video is not usable.
  • Every result shows its tier so recordings from different setups are never mixed up.
