About the certificate warning

When you open the HTTPS address, Chrome shows Your connection is not private. That is expected for this local prototype: the laptop made its own certificate instead of buying one, so Chrome cannot vouch for it. The connection is still encrypted, and it stays on your Wi-Fi.
